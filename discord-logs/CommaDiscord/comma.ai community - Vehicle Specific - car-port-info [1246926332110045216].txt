# Welcome to car porting!

* car ports live in the [opendbc repo](https://github.com/commaai/opendbc)
* [comma.ai/vehicles](https://comma.ai/vehicles) is an up-to-date list of (plug and play) supported cars
* WIP car ports are listed [here](https://github.com/orgs/commaai/projects/28)
* Not sure why you're car isn't supported? Find out more [here](https://github.com/commaai/openpilot/blob/master/docs/CARS.md#dont-see-your-car-here)

**Interested in porting your car?**
It's easier than it seems - porting a car is doable in most cases by anyone with good programming skills.

The difficulty is a function of the architecture of the car and how new it is to openpilot. In the best case, it's a weekend of hacking for someone new or an hour for an experienced car porter. @jyoung8607 has a good intro talk on the process: https://youtu.be/XxPS5TpTUnI

Every car port is eligible for a bounty: https://github.com/commaai/openpilot/blob/master/docs/BOUNTIES.md#car-bounties
